How much does a kitchen remodel cost in Miami, FL?

A kitchen remodel in Miami typically costs between $16,200 and $86,400, with most homeowners paying around $43,200. Local construction costs in Miami run above the national average, so prices here differ from the U.S. typical of $40,000.
