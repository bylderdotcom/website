How much does a kitchen remodel cost in Boston, MA?

A kitchen remodel in Boston typically costs between $18,800 and $100,000, with most homeowners paying around $50,000. Local construction costs in Boston run well above the national average, so prices here differ from the U.S. typical of $40,000.

What drives kitchen remodel cost in Boston

The biggest factors are project size and scope, the materials and finishes you choose, structural or permit work, and your contractor's rates. Because labor and materials in Boston, MA are well above the national average, the same project can cost noticeably more or less than in other metros.
