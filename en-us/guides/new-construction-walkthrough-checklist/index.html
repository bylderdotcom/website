New-construction walkthrough checklist

Updated June 2026 · free & printable · for U.S. new-home buyers

How to use this: Bring it to your final (blue-tape) walkthrough and mark every defect with painter's tape as you go. Anything you miss now can become your problem after closing. Tip: print this page or press Ctrl/Cmd + P.

Before the walkthrough

  • Bring your purchase agreement and the list of options/upgrades you paid for
  • Bring a phone (photos), a phone charger to test outlets, blue painter's tape, and a notepad
  • Schedule enough time — 1.5 to 3 hours; don't let it be rushed
  • Consider an independent third-party inspector in addition to the builder walkthrough

Every room

  • Walls & ceilings: no cracks, dents, nail pops, or visible drywall seams
  • Paint: even coverage, clean edges, no drips or missed spots
  • Floors: no scratches, gaps, lifting, squeaks, or hollow tiles
  • Windows: open, close, lock, and seal; no cracked glass or broken screens
  • Doors: swing freely, latch, don't rub the frame or floor
  • Outlets & switches: test each one (phone charger); confirm GFCI outlets trip and reset
  • Lights & fans: every fixture works; switches control the right fixtures
  • Trim & baseboards: tight joints, caulked, no gaps

Kitchen & bathrooms

  • Cabinets & drawers: align, open/close smoothly, no damage; hardware installed
  • Countertops: no chips, scratches, or seams that don't line up
  • Run every faucet: hot & cold, good pressure, no leaks under sinks
  • Flush every toilet; check for rocking or running
  • Showers/tubs: drain properly, no leaks, caulk lines clean
  • Appliances: power on and run a cycle (dishwasher, disposal, range, microwave)
  • Ventilation/exhaust fans work

Systems

  • HVAC: heat AND cooling both run; check the thermostat and air from every vent
  • Water heater: hot water at every fixture; label and capacity match the spec
  • Electrical panel: labeled, breakers correct, no exposed wiring
  • Smoke & CO detectors installed and working in required locations
  • Garage door opens/closes; auto-reverse safety works

Exterior

  • Grading slopes away from the foundation (no water pooling toward the house)
  • Siding/brick/stucco: no cracks, gaps, or damage; caulking complete
  • Roof & gutters: no missing shingles; downspouts direct water away
  • Driveway, walkways, patio: no major cracks or uneven settling
  • Exterior doors & garage: seal properly; weatherstripping intact
  • Hose bibs work; exterior outlets are GFCI and weather-covered

Verify what you paid for

  • Every upgrade and option on your contract is installed — and is the right one
  • Correct flooring, cabinets, countertops, fixtures, and finishes per your selections
  • Structural/electrical options you ordered (extra outlets, rough-ins, bump-outs) are present

Documents to get before closing

  • Written punch list of every defect, signed by the builder, with fix dates
  • Builder warranty documents (typically 1-year workmanship / 2-year systems / 10-year structural)
  • Appliance manuals and warranty registrations
  • List of subcontractors/brands for future service

Frequently asked questions

What is a blue-tape walkthrough?

It's the final pre-closing inspection of a new home where you mark every defect with blue painter's tape so the builder fixes them before you take ownership.

Should I hire my own inspector for a brand-new home?

Yes. Independent inspectors routinely find framing, HVAC, electrical, and waterproofing issues a buyer walkthrough misses — cheap insurance on a major purchase.

What if I find problems after closing?

Use your builder's warranty (commonly 1/2/10 years) and submit issues in writing. That's exactly why you keep the signed punch list and warranty documents.
