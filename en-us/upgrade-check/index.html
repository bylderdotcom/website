Questions

Which upgrades should I take from the builder?

Anything structural or inside walls, floors and ceilings: rough-in plumbing, extra electrical and recessed lighting, low-voltage wiring, gas stub-outs and structural options. These are costly or disruptive to add later. More in are builder upgrades worth it?

When should I get the check?

Before your design center appointment, or at the latest before the builder's deadline for options. Some builders set an earlier deadline for structural options than for finishes, so ask your builder for both dates.

Can my own contractor quote on it?

Yes. Invite any company by email. They see your floor plan and the options you picked, and their quote shows up next to the builder's design center price. You decide per company what they can see.

Is this the same as the free AI check?

No. The free AI check flags obvious overpricing in seconds. The Upgrade Check is a full review by a specialist, with the after-closing plan and local prices.
