How much does a flooring installation cost in Kansas City, MO?

A flooring installation in Kansas City typically costs between $2,700 and $18,000, with most homeowners paying around $7,200. Local construction costs in Kansas City run below the national average, so prices here differ from the U.S. typical of $8,000.
