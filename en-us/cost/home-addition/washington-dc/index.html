How much does a home addition cost in Washington, DC?

A home addition in Washington typically costs between $28,700 and $172,500, with most homeowners paying around $69,000. Local construction costs in Washington run well above the national average, so prices here differ from the U.S. typical of $60,000.

What drives home addition cost in Washington

The biggest factors are project size and scope, the materials and finishes you choose, structural or permit work, and your contractor's rates. Because labor and materials in Washington, DC are well above the national average, the same project can cost noticeably more or less than in other metros.
