How much does a siding replacement cost in Seattle, WA?

A siding replacement in Seattle typically costs between $8,400 and $42,000, with most homeowners paying around $18,000. Local construction costs in Seattle run well above the national average, so prices here differ from the U.S. typical of $15,000.
