How much does a hvac replacement cost in Charlotte, NC?

A hvac replacement in Charlotte typically costs between $4,600 and $16,700, with most homeowners paying around $9,300. Local construction costs in Charlotte run below the national average, so prices here differ from the U.S. typical of $10,000.
